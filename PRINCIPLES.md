# HQ Core Principles

These principles govern how the HQ platform, its treasury, and the HQ Token
are built and operated. They exist to keep the project grounded in a
sustainable business first, and a token ecosystem second.

## 1. Revenue First
Never build token mechanics before sustainable advertising revenue exists.

## 2. Treasury Backed
HQ Token must be backed by business-generated treasury, not speculation.

## 3. Credit is Internal
Credits are not cryptocurrency and do not represent a fixed cash value.

## 4. Delayed Tokenization
Token functionality is introduced only after Phase 1 has demonstrated
sustainable revenue and operational stability.

## 5. Sustainable Token Economy
Treasury funds operations, referrals, liquidity, and strategic reserves.
Token distribution should never exceed what the business can sustainably
support.

## 6. Utility Before Speculation
HQ Token must have meaningful platform utility before encouraging exchange
trading.

## 7. Long-Term Growth
The platform exists to build a sustainable business first and a token
ecosystem second.
